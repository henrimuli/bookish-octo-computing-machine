# Launch Directory — Product & Build Plan

> Working repo name: `bookish-octo-computing-machine` (temporary).
> Suggested product name: **LaunchLedger** (see §6 for alternatives).
> Research date: October 2026. Prices below were gathered from the directories' own pages where search surfaced them, otherwise from third-party reviews. Directory pricing changes often and sources conflicted, so treat every figure as "approximately, as of Oct 2026."

---

## 1. Executive summary

The launch-directory niche has stopped being "Product Hunt clones." The sites that make money now sell four things:

1. **Time.** Free launches sit in a queue that runs from 2 weeks to 6+ months. Paying skips it. This is the core revenue line for TinyLaunch, Uneed, MicroLaunch, DevHunt, OpenHunts and PeerPush.
2. **A dofollow backlink from a high-DR domain.** Free tiers trade the link for a badge on the maker's site ("pay in link equity"). Paid tiers drop the badge requirement. This is also how the directories build their own DR, because every free listing links back to them.
3. **Attention.** Homepage/featured slots, sidebar ads, newsletter sponsorships, and "Product of the Day/Week" badges. These are sold weekly or monthly.
4. **Done-for-you submissions.** Nick Launches, TinyLaunch, SaaSCity, Smol Launch and Uneed all sell manual submission to 30–180 other directories for $99–$299. It's the highest-ticket product in the niche, and most vendors deliver it as a PDF/spreadsheet report at the end.

**Where the gap is:** nobody does submission tracking well. ListingBott and others send a final report. Founders complain that directories quietly flip links to `nofollow`, drop listings, or never publish. A live dashboard that monitors every placement (status, live URL, rel attribute, indexation, referral clicks) and keeps checking after the job ends is a real differentiator. It can also become a subscription.

**Positioning:** "Launch, get listed everywhere, and see proof." A weekly launch board with honest voting, plus a submission service whose dashboard is a ledger of every link you own. That's where the name comes from.

**What the market expects:** manual (hand) submissions, high-DR targets, dofollow focus, 1–5 week turnaround, an honest "we submit, we can't guarantee acceptance" disclaimer, and a report. **What nobody convincingly offers:** a live per-directory dashboard plus ongoing link monitoring after delivery.

### 2.3 Patterns that recur everywhere

- **Badge-for-backlink free tier.** The maker embeds a badge that links to their listing. The directory checks it (usually by fetching the homepage server-side) and grants a dofollow link while the badge stays. Removing it reverts the link to `nofollow` or delists. Pain points from founders: bot protection (Cloudflare) makes the badge look missing, and rel attributes flip silently. **DNS TXT verification** is a cleaner alternative some offer.
- **Artificial scarcity.** Weekly caps on free slots (SaaSCity: 15/week), capped paid slots (MicroLaunch: 40/month), and long free queues create the reason to pay.
- **Winner badges as distribution.** Top-3 badges for Day/Week/Month spread the directory's brand onto winners' sites. Every badge is a backlink.
- **Recaps and a blog.** Weekly winner recap posts plus "best X" / "alternatives to X" SEO content. Nick Launches' directory database is the best example of a lead magnet.
- **Recurring vs. one-time.** Most sell one-time launches. Startup Fame shows monthly subscriptions work when placement is ongoing.
- **AI discovery.** PeerPush and SaaSCity are pitching visibility in LLM answers (`llms.txt`, MCP server, structured data). This is new as of 2026 and worth matching early.

### 2.4 Founder complaints to design against

- Traffic is a 24h spike of other founders, not customers.
- Vote rings and launch crews ($1.5–2k anecdotally). PH dampens bursts from new accounts.
- "I submitted a link and never got anything back": no stats, no feedback.
- "Dofollow" promises that turn out to be `nofollow` (one DEV.to audit found about half the directories it checked served nofollow).
- Badge checks failing because of bot protection.
- Queues of 6–60 weeks with no visibility into position.

## 3. Product definition

### 3.1 Core loop

```
Submit product → (free: verify badge → enter queue) | (paid: pick date)
      → Launch week (Mon 00:00 UTC → Sun 23:59 UTC) → community votes + comments
      → Weekly winners (top 3) + Day/Month/Year boards → winner badges + recap post + newsletter
      → Permanent listing page (SEO, alternatives, categories) → upsell submission service + monitoring
```

**Launch cadence: weekly** (like SaaSCity, TinyLaunch, Peerlist and Nick Launches). It gives small products a fair 7-day window instead of a 24h fight, and at our launch volume it gives each vote more weight. A daily "Top today" view still exists inside the week for freshness. **Monthly and yearly boards** (Uneed-style) give extra winner badges.

### 3.2 Feature list

**Listings**
- Product page: name, tagline, logo, gallery (images + video embed), description (Markdown), categories (max 3), tags, pricing model, platforms, website + social links, makers (multi-maker teams), launch date, vote count, comments, "alternatives to", related products.
- Free listings stay up permanently.
- Category pages, tag pages, "Alternatives to X" pages, comparison pages (later, SEO).
- Search with filters (category, pricing model, launch date, sort by votes/new).
- Structured data (`SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD), OG images generated per product, `llms.txt`, sitemap.

**Free tier with badge verification**
- Maker embeds a badge snippet (`<a href="https://launchledger.com/p/slug?ref=badge"><img src=".../badge/slug.svg"></a>`).
- Verification methods: (1) **server-side fetch** of the homepage, then parse for the anchor to our domain; (2) **DNS TXT** record fallback for bot-protected sites; (3) a meta tag fallback.
- After verification, the listing link is `rel="dofollow"`. A **re-check runs weekly**. If the badge is missing: email + 7-day grace period, then the link downgrades to `nofollow` (we don't delist; that's friendlier and keeps the page's SEO). It auto-restores when the badge returns.
- Badge variants: "Launching on", "Featured on", "#1/#2/#3 Product of the Week/Month", light/dark, SVG served with live vote count.
- Free launches are **capped per week** (start at 20/week; tune it) and enter a **visible queue with position + ETA** in the dashboard. Founders complain that other sites hide this.

**Paid launch tiers** (§5): skip the queue, pick a date, no badge required, featured placement, newsletter and social inclusion, launch article.

**Voting & community**
- Upvotes (one per account per product), comments with threads, maker replies flagged.
- Auth required to vote (email magic link, Google, GitHub, X).
- **Anti-fraud:** account-age weighting (accounts under 72h or with no activity count at a fractional weight for ranking), velocity/burst detection, IP/ASN/device fingerprint clustering, disposable-email blocklist, and vote auditing. Flagged votes are kept but excluded from ranking. Publish a short "how ranking works" page; transparency is a selling point.
- Ranking score = weighted votes + small weight for substantive comments (PH-style), computed by a job.

**Winners**
- Weekly top 3 (Gold/Silver/Bronze), plus monthly and yearly.
- Automated: a cron closes the week, freezes the ranking, issues badges, emails winners, and drafts the **recap blog post** + newsletter.
- Winners get a **permanent dofollow link regardless of tier** (Startups Lab model). This rewards free-tier makers who win.

**Featured & ads**
- Homepage "Featured" rail (fixed number of slots, sold weekly).
- Sidebar/category sponsor slots.
- Newsletter sponsorship.
- Self-serve slot booking calendar showing availability (scarcity is visible), with Stripe checkout.
- Click tracking on every featured slot, reported to the buyer. Optional TinyLaunch-style CPC guarantee: if CPC exceeds the threshold, the slot extends for free.

**Maker dashboard**
- My products, launch status, queue position/ETA, badge verification status + last check, link rel status.
- Analytics per product: page views, outbound clicks (with UTM `?ref=launchledger`), votes over time, referrers, badge impressions.
- Upgrade flows.

**Directory submission service + tracking dashboard** (the differentiator; see §4).

**Content & growth**
- **Public directory database** (Nick Launches-style): 150–250 launch directories with DR, traffic band, link type (verified by our own crawler), pricing, queue length, niche. Filterable. This is the main SEO magnet and the top of the funnel for the submission service.
- Weekly recap posts, launch guides, "best X tools" roundups.
- Newsletter (weekly winners + new launches + sponsor).
- Free tools: "Dofollow checker" (paste a listing URL and we report the rel), badge generator, launch checklist. All lead magnets.

**Admin**
- Moderation queue (approve/reject listings with reasons), vote-fraud review, comment moderation, featured-slot calendar, ads inventory, refunds, submission-service ops console (§4), directory database editor, CMS for blog posts.

## 4. Directory submission service + tracking dashboard

### 4.1 Service

- **Manual submissions** by an ops team (contractors) using a prepared product kit: logo variants, screenshots, 3 description lengths (60/160/500 chars), categories, founder info, and a dedicated submission email inbox per client (so verification emails are captured).
- Target list = our curated directory database, scored by DR, verified link type, acceptance rate, and turnaround. That data comes from our own outcomes over time, which is a moat.
- Clear terms: "We submit to N directories. Typical acceptance 60–80%. Paid placements inside third-party directories aren't included unless stated." Free resubmission to replacements if a directory is dead.

### 4.2 Tracking dashboard (client-facing)

Per order, one row per target directory:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Directory, DR, category | from our DB |
| Status | `queued → submitted → pending review → live → rejected / needs action / dead` |
| Submitted at, live at | timestamps, SLA timers |
| Live URL | link to the listing on the third-party site |
| Link check | rel (`dofollow`/`nofollow`/`ugc`/`sponsored`), anchor text, HTTP status, last checked |
| Indexed? | Google index check (search-console style or via a SERP API) |
| Referral clicks | needs the client to connect GA4/Plausible, or we use UTM'd URLs + our redirect domain where the directory allows it |
| Evidence | screenshot captured at publish time (Playwright) |
| Notes / action needed | e.g. "Directory asks for reciprocal badge", "Email verification needed" |

Plus: progress bar, ETA, acceptance rate, DR-weighted "link value" summary, exportable CSV/PDF report, shareable read-only link (agencies love this), and email digests on status changes.

### 4.3 Ongoing monitoring (recurring revenue)

After delivery, a crawler re-checks every live placement weekly (status code, presence, rel attribute) and alerts on changes: "3 links flipped to nofollow, 1 listing removed." The client can also add links they placed themselves. This is the **Link Monitor** subscription (§5). It turns a one-time $149 buyer into a $9–19/month subscriber and fixes the market's top complaint.

### 4.4 Ops console (internal)

- Kanban per order and per directory, assignment to contractors, per-directory submission instructions/checklist + saved form answers, captcha/email-verification handling notes, time tracking, QA step (a second person verifies the live URL), and bulk status updates.
- A directory health score that updates automatically from outcomes (acceptance rate, time-to-live, link type observed), which feeds back into target selection.
- Semi-automation later: browser-extension autofill (Nick Launches ships an extension) to speed up manual submissions. Keep a human in the loop. Many directories ban bots, and "hand-done" is a selling point.

## 5. Pricing recommendation

The market clusters at: **$15–40** to skip a queue, **$50–120** for featured/top placement, **$99–299** for submissions, **$15–60/week** for featured slots, **$19–149/month** for subscriptions. Price one notch under the leaders to start, with launch discounts, then raise prices once there's DR and traffic. We will have neither at launch, which is the honest constraint (see §8).

|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Listing + launch week from the queue (visible position/ETA), capped slots/week. Dofollow link **while badge is verified**. Eligible for winner badges. Winners keep dofollow permanently. |

## 6. Name

**Primary recommendation: LaunchLedger**
- It says what's different: a ledger of every launch and every link, with proof. That fits the tracking dashboard and the verified-link angle.
- It reads clearly, alliterates, and works for both sides (directory + service): "Launch on LaunchLedger", "Featured on LaunchLedger", "Tracked by LaunchLedger".
- Badge copy: *"#1 Product of the Week — LaunchLedger"*.

**Alternatives**

| Name | Angle |
|---|---|
| **Shipboard** | Leaderboard + "ship it". Short and memorable. Probably contested |
| **Launchyard** | Shipyard metaphor: where products get built and launched |
| **Debutly** | "Debut" = launch. Friendly and brandable |
| **Hatchlist** | Launch + list. Indie feel |
| **Liftlog** | Lift-off + log (tracking angle). Short |
| **ProofLaunch** | Leads with "proof" (verified links, real stats) |

I couldn't check domain or trademark availability. This environment's network policy blocks direct site and registrar lookups. Before settling: check `.com` / `.io` / `.so`, search USPTO/EUIPO, and check X/GitHub handles. Avoid names close to existing players: LaunchPad, LaunchPanda, LaunchIgniter, LaunchBuff, LaunchReceipt (a link-check site, close in concept to "Ledger"; check for confusion), Launching Next, LaunchList, Smol Launch.

## 7. Technical plan

### 7.1 Stack

| Layer | Choice | Why |

| UI | Tailwind + shadcn/ui | Fast, consistent |

## 8. Go-to-market (the hard part)

The product is easy to build. The niche is crowded (200+ launch sites), and **DR and traffic are what customers are really buying.** A new domain starts at DR 0, so:

1. **Seed DR before charging premium prices.** Submit our own site to the 150+ directories in our DB (dogfooding the service, which also generates the first case study and directory data). List on the top launch boards (PH, Uneed, TinyLaunch, Fazier, Startup Fame...).
2. **The badge flywheel.** Every free launch adds a badge link to us. Keep free generous early (higher weekly cap) to grow referring domains fast.
3. **Directory database as the SEO magnet.** Publish 200 directory profile pages with real, crawler-verified link types ("we checked: actually nofollow"). That's unique, linkable data competitors don't verify.
4. **Weekly recap posts + newsletter** from day one. Winners share their badges and recaps.
5. **Launch the submission service early.** It doesn't depend on our DR, so it can bring in revenue before the board has traffic.
6. **Build in public** on X/Indie Hackers with MRR (TrustMRR), which is how this niche's own founders grow.
7. **AI visibility:** `llms.txt`, clean JSON-LD, and optionally an MCP server exposing the catalogue (SaaSCity does).

Pricing note: until DR is above ~50, keep launch tiers at the low end ($9–19 promos) and lean on the submission service + monitoring for revenue.

## 9. Roadmap

**Phase 0 — Foundations (week 1)**
Repo setup, Next.js + Drizzle + Postgres, auth, Stripe test mode, CI (lint/typecheck/test), base design system, final name + domain.

**Phase 1 — MVP launch board (weeks 2–4)**
Product submission + moderation, product pages, categories, weekly launch weeks + free queue (with visible ETA), voting + comments, basic anti-fraud (auth, Turnstile, account-age weighting), weekly winners job + badges, badge verification (HTML + DNS), paid tiers (Fast/Pro/Premium/Spotlight) via Stripe, maker dashboard v1, SEO basics (sitemap, JSON-LD, OG images), transactional email.

**Phase 2 — Monetization surface (weeks 5–6)**
Featured slot calendar + self-serve booking, click/impression stats, newsletter, blog/CMS + automated recap drafts, monthly/yearly boards, pricing + advertise pages.

**Phase 3 — Submission service + tracking (weeks 7–9)**
Directory database (admin editor + public pages), submission order checkout, ops console (kanban, per-directory checklists, QA step), client tracking dashboard, evidence screenshots, link checks, CSV/PDF export, shareable reports, status-change emails.

**Phase 4 — Link Monitor & subscriptions (weeks 10–11)**
Tracked links for any URL, weekly monitoring + alerts, Maker Pro + Always Featured + Link Monitor subscriptions, affiliate program, free tools (dofollow checker, badge generator).

**Phase 5 — Scale & moat (ongoing)**
Advanced vote-fraud detection, "alternatives"/comparison SEO pages, agency plan + white-label, browser extension for ops autofill, index checking, `llms.txt`/MCP server, gamified homepage element (a SaaSCity-style hook, optional), public API.
